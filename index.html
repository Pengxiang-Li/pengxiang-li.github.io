Pengxiang LI

Email: pengxiangli1999[at]gmail[dot]com

I am a third-year PhD student in Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT), advised by Dr. Yuwei Wu and Dr. Yunde Jia. I am also a member of the joint PhD program (‘TONG Program’) with Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI), and I am grateful to be advised by Dr. Qing Li and Dr. Zhi Gao. Currently, I am an intern at Alibaba Tongyi Lab, where I work on online reinforcement learning (RL) for base models. Previously, I got my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from BIT in 2021.

My research interests lie in Agentic RL, GUI/Tool-Use/OpenClaw Agents, Multimodal Agents, Non-Euclidean Optimization, and 3DV Understanding. Feel free to reach out if you are interested in my work! 🤝
